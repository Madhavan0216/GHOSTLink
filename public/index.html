ℹ️ About GHOSTLINK

GHOSTLINK

ONION V1

Version: 1.0.0

Engine: Tor 0.4.8.10

Privacy: Tor Browser-level parity

GHOSTLINK routes all traffic through the Tor network, providing anonymous browsing with first-party isolation, HTTPS-First mode, and automatic data clearing on disconnect.

⚠️ Remember: No system provides 100% anonymity. Practice safe browsing habits.
